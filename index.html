1. Executive Summary

This report proposes POLTEN as the flagship product and packaging brand of Dungsam Polymers Limited (DPL). POLTEN is a strategically developed brand name that combines industrial strength, polymer technology, Bhutanese identity, and future business scalability.

The name is designed to support the company's long-term growth ambitions in packaging, industrial textiles, advanced polymer products, sustainable materials, and export markets.

Unlike conventional industry names limited to a specific product or local reference, POLTEN provides a strong foundation for building a globally recognized Bhutanese industrial brand.

POLTEN is positioned as the flagship industrial packaging brand of DPL, representing strength, reliability, and innovation in polymer-based packaging solutions, while maintaining a strong Bhutanese identity and global commercial appeal.

3. Brand Story

Bhutan is entering a new era of industrial development. As industries grow, infrastructure expands, and trade increases, the need for reliable packaging solutions becomes increasingly important. Behind every bag of cement, every agricultural product, every feed product, and every industrial shipment lies a simple requirement: strength.

POLTEN was created to symbolize that strength. The name reflects the company's expertise in polymer technology while honoring Bhutanese values of resilience, trust, and long-term sustainability.

POLTEN is more than a product name. It represents a commitment to protecting products, supporting industries, enabling trade, and contributing to Bhutan's economic growth. Every POLTEN product is designed to carry value safely, efficiently, and reliably from manufacturer to customer.

12. Anticipated Questions

Why not use a Bhutan-specific name?
A purely Bhutan-centric name may limit international market perception. POLTEN balances Bhutanese identity with global competitiveness.
Why not use Dungsam as the product brand?
Corporate brands and product brands serve different purposes. POLTEN allows DPL to develop a strong consumer and industrial market identity while preserving corporate credibility.
Will the brand become outdated?
No. POLTEN is technology-based rather than product-based, enabling long-term relevance even as product lines evolve.
Can the brand support exports?
Yes. The name is simple, memorable, and internationally acceptable.
